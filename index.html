About

Hello, I'm Lina.

I'm a Bay Area photographer with a quiet, documentary approach to portraits. I believe the best photographs aren't posed — they're felt. My work lives somewhere between film stillness and morning light.

Originally from Philadelphia, I've called the Bay home since 2016. When I'm not behind a camera, you'll find me enjoying time with friends and family in community, traveling to new places, or working my day job in tech.
